## Intelligence & Reporting page

The Intelligence & Reporting page brings machine-learning forecasting, reconciliation intelligence, risk scoring and report orchestration together across the platform. It shows open anomalies, forecast accuracy, the average risk score and reconciled records.

## Intelligence Pipeline

The intelligence pipeline runs anomaly detection, forecasting, compliance risk scoring, reconciliation intelligence, reporting orchestration, decision support and migration integrity. Select a step on the page to see its detail.

## Real-Time Anomaly Detection

Real-Time Anomaly Detection flags statistical drift and unusual transactions as they happen, so they can be reviewed before they cause harm.

## Predictive Forecasting

Predictive Forecasting projects cash flow and operational figures from several variables, and tracks how accurate each forecasting model has been.

## Compliance Risk Scoring

Compliance Risk Scoring gives each entity a dynamic risk score from its compliance posture and risk factors.

## Reconciliation Intelligence

Reconciliation Intelligence suggests matches using fuzzy matching and helps resolve exceptions; the final reconciliation stays governed and recorded.

## Reporting Orchestrations

Reporting Orchestrations run scheduled and on-demand report batches across the platform.

## Decision Support

Decision Support offers algorithmic recommendations and impact analyses at approval and oversight points, while the decision stays with the people approving.

## Migration Integrity

Migration Integrity checks a cutover from a legacy system with checksums and ledger parity, so migrated data can be shown to match its source.
