## Delegated Authority

Delegated Authority is a register of who may act for whom: time-bound, entity-scoped grants of authority.

## Time-bound and entity-scoped

Each grant names the person, the entity it applies to and the period it is valid for, so approvals can be checked against authority that was in force at the time.
