## Service Input & Execution Console

The Service Input & Execution Console, under Platform then Service Inputs, lists every microservice by domain with its endpoint and port, and lets administrators send test inputs and watch health. It shows overall service health, dispatched tests and response times, and can be filtered by domain.

## Run and check services

Service Inputs can refresh service health or send test inputs to services.
To refresh health or execute services:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Platform**, then select **Service Inputs**.
3. Select **Refresh Health** to re-check every service, or **Expand All Inputs** to open every service's test input.
4. Select **Execute All Services** to send each service its test input.
5. Use **View domain** on a service to open its page.
Overall service health shows which services are running.
