## What ZoikoSuite is

ZoikoSuite is a governance-first business operations platform connecting finance, workforce, legal, tax, compliance, procurement and evidence workflows. The precise capabilities available depend on market, plan and deployment status. It is built so that material business actions, such as a payment, a contract or a filing, go through shared approvals and keep a record of how each decision was made.

## How the platform is organized

Its business areas cover finance and payments, payroll and human resources, tax, legal and contracts, compliance, and commercial operations such as procurement. A shared governance layer provides policies, approvals, delegated authority, evidence requirements and a decision log that the areas share. A platform foundation provides identity, tenant and legal entity structure, access control and notifications.

## How governance runs through the work

Before a material action proceeds, the platform checks whether the person may take it, which policy applies, which approvals are needed and what evidence must exist. Each decision is recorded with its context, so a team can later see who acted, on what basis and when. Approval workflows add to these permission checks rather than replacing them.

## What depends on your setup

Which areas, workflows and integrations you can use depends on your market, plan and deployment. ZoikoSuite supports finance, legal and tax professionals in their work and does not give legal, tax or financial advice.
