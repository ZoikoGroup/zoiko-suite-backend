## Draft a contract

Draft a contract starts a new contract. A draft is versioned from the start and moves through approval before it becomes active.
To draft a contract:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Legal, Corporate & Commercial**, then select **Legal & Contracts**.
3. Go to **Draft a contract**.
4. Enter the **Contract title**, the **Type**, the **Counterparty ID** and **Counterparty name**.
5. Set **Effective from** and **Effective to**, the **Contract value** and **Currency**, and a **Description**.
6. Select **Draft contract**.

## Contract register

The Contract register lists your organization's contracts with their status, and opens each one as a contract record.

## Lifecycle

A contract's Lifecycle offers only the transitions that are valid from where it stands, and the platform checks each transition again when it arrives.
To move a contract through its lifecycle:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Legal, Corporate & Commercial**, then select **Legal & Contracts**.
3. Open the contract from the **Contract register**.
4. In **Lifecycle**, choose the transition offered, such as **Approve**, **Record execution**, **Amend**, **Renew** or **Terminate**.
5. Fill in what it asks for, such as who signed or the termination reason, and confirm.
Each transition adds a snapshot to the contract's version history.

## Terms

Terms shows the contract's terms as currently stored, at its current version.

## Version history

Old contract versions are kept: Version history shows permanent snapshots of a contract, oldest first. A snapshot is added on drafting, revision, activation and termination, so every earlier state of the contract can still be read.

## Legal & Contracts page

The Legal & Contracts page shows active contracts, the clause library, tracked obligations and board resolutions, with panels for Contract Lifecycle Management, Clause Library & Template Governance, Legal Obligation Tracking, Board Governance & Resolutions, and Corporate Actions & Counterparty Risk. The buttons at the top are **Create Contract**, **Draft Clause** and **Propose Resolution**.

## Legal Lifecycle & Contract Governance Flow

The Legal lifecycle runs in this order: a template is drafted, its clauses are redlined, the contract gets legal approval, the counterparty is screened, the obligations it creates are tracked, and a board resolution is recorded where one is needed.

## Create Contract

Create Contract registers a new contract draft. A draft is versioned from the start and moves through approval before it becomes active.
To create a contract:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Legal, Corporate & Commercial**, then select **Legal & Contracts**.
3. At the top of the page, select **Create Contract**.
4. Enter the **Contract Title**, such as Enterprise Cloud MSA for a customer.
5. Choose the **Contract Type**: MSA (Master Services Agreement), NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) or SLA (Service Level Agreement).
6. Select **Create Contract Draft**.
The draft then appears under **Contract Lifecycle Management**, where it moves through approval, execution and renewal.

## Clause Library & Template Governance

The Clause Library holds pre-vetted clauses and templates, such as indemnity and liability terms, so contracts are drafted from approved wording.
To draft a clause:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Legal, Corporate & Commercial**, then select **Legal & Contracts**.
3. At the top of the page, select **Draft Clause**.
4. Enter the **Clause Title**, such as Confidentiality Clause.
5. Choose the **Category**: Confidentiality, Indemnification, Termination, Liability, Governing Law, Payment Terms or Other.
6. Enter the **Clause Body**, the clause text itself.
7. Select **Create Clause**.

## Board Governance & Resolutions

Board Governance & Resolutions records board meetings, voting and the resolutions a board passes.
To propose a board resolution:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Legal, Corporate & Commercial**, then select **Legal & Contracts**.
3. At the top of the page, select **Propose Resolution**.
4. Enter the **Resolution title** and choose the **Meeting it is put to**, or no meeting for a standalone resolution.
5. Choose **What kind of decision is this?**, and add the **Resolution number** and the date the **Decision applies from**.
6. Enter what the resolution says, then select **Propose resolution**.
A resolution is tracked from proposal through approval, execution and archiving.

## Schedule a board meeting

A board meeting is scheduled first so resolutions can be put to it.
To schedule a board meeting:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Legal, Corporate & Commercial**, then select **Legal & Contracts**.
3. In **Board Governance & Resolutions**, go to the meeting form.
4. Enter the **Meeting title**, when it is **Scheduled at**, the **Location**, and the date the **Record applies from**.
5. Select **Schedule meeting**.

## Legal Obligation Tracking

Legal Obligation Tracking follows the obligations contracts create: compliance milestones, audit triggers and service-level deadlines, each linked back to the contract and clause it came from.

## Corporate Actions & Counterparty Risk

Corporate Actions & Counterparty Risk records entity changes and share issuances, and verifies counterparties, including their ultimate beneficial owners, before they are relied on.
