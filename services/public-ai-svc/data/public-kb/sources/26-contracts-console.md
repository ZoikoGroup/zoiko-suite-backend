## Draft a contract

Draft a contract starts a new contract with its counterparty, type, value and terms. A draft is versioned from the start and moves through approval before it becomes active.

## Contract register

The Contract register lists your organization's contracts with their status, and opens each one as a contract record.

## Lifecycle

A contract's Lifecycle offers only the transitions that are valid from where it stands, such as submitting for approval, activating or terminating. The platform checks each transition again when it arrives.

## Terms

Terms shows the contract's terms as currently stored, at its current version.

## Version history

Old contract versions are kept: Version history shows permanent snapshots of a contract, oldest first. A snapshot is added on drafting, revision, activation and termination, so every earlier state of the contract can still be read.
