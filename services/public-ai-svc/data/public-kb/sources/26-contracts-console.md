## Draft a contract

Draft a contract starts a new contract. A draft is versioned from the start and moves through approval before it becomes active.
To draft a contract:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Legal, Corporate & Commercial**, then select **Legal & Contracts**.
3. Go to **Draft a contract**.
4. Enter the **Contract title**, the **Type**, the **Counterparty ID** and **Counterparty name**.
5. Set **Effective from** and **Effective to**, the **Contract value** and **Currency**, and a **Description**.
6. Select **Draft contract**.

## Contract register

The Contract register lists your organization's contracts with their status, and opens each one as a contract record.

## Lifecycle

A contract's Lifecycle offers only the transitions that are valid from where it stands, and the platform checks each transition again when it arrives.
To move a contract through its lifecycle:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Legal, Corporate & Commercial**, then select **Legal & Contracts**.
3. Open the contract from the **Contract register**.
4. In **Lifecycle**, choose the transition offered, such as **Approve**, **Record execution**, **Amend**, **Renew** or **Terminate**.
5. Fill in what it asks for, such as who signed or the termination reason, and confirm.
Each transition adds a snapshot to the contract's version history.

## Terms

Terms shows the contract's terms as currently stored, at its current version.

## Version history

Old contract versions are kept: Version history shows permanent snapshots of a contract, oldest first. A snapshot is added on drafting, revision, activation and termination, so every earlier state of the contract can still be read.
