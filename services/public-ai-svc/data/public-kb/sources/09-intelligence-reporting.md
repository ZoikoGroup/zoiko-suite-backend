## Intelligence and Reporting

Intelligence and Reporting is the ZoikoSuite area for reporting across the other areas. It brings together reports and analysis built from the records the other areas keep, without changing those records.

## Reporting

Reporting coordinates executive, board, operational and regulatory reports from derived data. Reports are read from the other areas' records and never replace them as the authoritative source. Because each report draws on governed records, its content can be traced back to the area that owns it.

## Analysis

Anomaly detection flags unusual patterns across finance, payroll, compliance and workflow data, and can score them but not change them. Forecasting produces forward-looking tax, payroll, liability and operational forecasts.

## Recommendations

Reconciliation assistance suggests matches and helps prioritize exceptions. Decision support offers recommendations at approval and oversight points, while the decision stays with the people approving.

## What depends on your setup

Scores and recommendations support people's decisions and do not replace approvals. The reports and analysis available to you depend on market, plan and deployment status.
