## Compliance

Compliance manages statutory, regulatory, operational and internal policy obligations as a managed, evidential system. In the Compliance module, each obligation has an owner, a due point and the evidence that shows it was met, so compliance can be reviewed rather than reconstructed.

## How obligations are tracked

Every obligation is bound to an entity and a jurisdiction and can point back to its source, such as a contract clause, a filing rule, a policy mandate or a jurisdictional rule. Filings are tracked from due-date planning through submission and confirmation, and compliance status shows where each obligation and control stands.

## Exceptions and risk

Missed or blocked items become governed exception cases with escalation routing. Risk scoring ranks obligations, filings and control exposures by severity, proximity and entity impact, and anomaly detection can flag unusual patterns without changing operational records.

## What depends on your setup

Which obligations and jurisdictions are tracked depends on your configuration and current published coverage. Tracking an obligation does not by itself mean it has been met, and ZoikoSuite does not give legal or regulatory advice.
