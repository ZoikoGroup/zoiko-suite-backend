## Commercial Ops

Commercial Ops covers procurement, vendor approvals and invoice workflows. It links commercial execution to financial and legal controls, so buying decisions follow the same governance as the rest of the platform.

## Vendor and invoice workflows

Vendors are approved before they are used, and supplier invoices follow approval workflows before they are paid. Vendor due diligence covers validation, onboarding checks, and sanctions and risk status, and keeps the evidence of those checks. An invoice's approval state is settled before it is released for payment.

## Procurement and spend controls

Procurement routes each purchase through governed spend steps, and purchase orders are issued, amended and linked to fulfillment. Spend controls check a purchase against policy and track spending against the limits that policy sets.

## What depends on your setup

Approval thresholds, spend limits and vendor checks follow each organization's own policies. Vendor checks and invoice approvals keep their records, so reviewers can see why a vendor was approved or an invoice was released. The workflows available to you depend on market, plan and deployment status.
