## Compliance page

The Compliance page governs statutory, regulatory, operational and internal policy obligations as a managed, evidential system. It shows compliance evaluations, filing requirements, evidence manifests and escalated exceptions, and has **New Statutory Filing**, **Evaluate Compliance** and **Upload Evidence Manifest** at the top.

## Statutory Compliance Governance Flow

Statutory compliance follows one flow: the obligation is catalogued, its evidence standard is defined, the evidence manifest is verified, the deadline is checked, the filing is submitted, and any exception is audited.

## New Statutory Filing

New Statutory Filing registers a statutory filing requirement, such as an annual confirmation statement, so its deadline is tracked.
To create a new statutory filing:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Tax & Compliance**, then select **Compliance**.
3. At the top of the page, select **New Statutory Filing**.
4. Enter the **Filing Name** and the **Authority**, such as Companies House UK.
5. Set the **Due Date** and choose the **Filing Type**: VAT100 MTD, Corporation Tax, Annual Return or PAYE.
6. Select **Create Statutory Requirement**.

## Evaluate Compliance

Evaluate Compliance records a compliance evaluation for a jurisdiction: how many obligations there are and how many are fulfilled, pending or overdue.
To evaluate compliance:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Tax & Compliance**, then select **Compliance**.
3. At the top of the page, select **Evaluate Compliance**.
4. Choose the **Jurisdiction**: UK Companies House, US Federal or Singapore ACRA.
5. Enter the **Total Obligations** and how many are **Fulfilled**, **Pending** and **Overdue**.
6. Select **Run Compliance Evaluation**.

## Upload Evidence Manifest

An evidence manifest gathers the verified evidence for a compliance review, an audit, a regulator request or legal discovery.
To upload an evidence manifest:
1. Sign in to your ZoikoSuite account. If you do not have one yet, ask your organization's administrator for access.
2. In the left menu, open **Tax & Compliance**, then select **Compliance**.
3. At the top of the page, select **Upload Evidence Manifest**.
4. Choose the **Scenario Type**: Compliance Review, Audit, Regulator Request or Legal Discovery.
5. Enter who it is **Requested By**.
6. Select **Generate Evidence Manifest**.

## Statutory Filing Requirements

Statutory Filing Requirements lists the regulatory obligations and the evidence standard each one needs.

## Compliance Status & Exception Escalations

Compliance Status & Exception Escalations shows verified evidence manifests and tracks service-level breaches. Escalated Compliance Exceptions lists open breach tickets.

## Anomaly Detection Signals

Anomaly Detection Signals on the Compliance page are real-time pattern outliers in behaviour or the ledger, flagged for review. A signal never changes operational records.

## Decision Support & Audit Readiness Checklist

The Decision Support & Audit Readiness Checklist suggests improvements to audit posture and shows which statutory audit-readiness controls are verified.
