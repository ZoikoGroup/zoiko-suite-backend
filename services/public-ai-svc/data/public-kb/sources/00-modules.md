## ZoikoSuite core modules

ZoikoSuite's core modules are grouped into domains: Finance (Accounting and General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, and Treasury and Cash Position), Workforce (Payroll and Human Resources), Tax, Legal and Compliance (Tax Management, Legal and Contracts, and Compliance and Obligations), and Commercial (Procurement and Commercial Operations). The modules available to you depend on market, plan and deployment status.

## Governance Platform and Platform Foundation

Alongside the core modules, ZoikoSuite organizes its published capabilities into a Governance Platform, which provides shared approvals, evidence and audit across the modules, and a Platform Foundation.
