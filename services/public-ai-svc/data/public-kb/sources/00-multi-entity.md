## Multi-entity operations

ZoikoSuite describes multi-entity operations and governed controls. Whether a particular consolidation or intercompany workflow is available for your organization depends on the approved plan and implementation scope. It is designed for groups that operate multiple entities, each with its own records, jurisdiction and approvals.

## How the group is structured

ZoikoSuite keeps an authoritative structure of legal entities, their hierarchies and reporting relationships, and the jurisdiction, fiscal and currency defaults of each one. Every material record resolves back to its owner in that structure, so work stays tied to the company it belongs to.

## Intercompany and consolidation

Intercompany entries are governed, matched and balanced between companies in the group instead of being merged into one company's records. Consolidated views are derived from each company's own records and do not replace them.

## Controls for each company

Delegated authority is granted for a specific company, and obligations are bound to the company and jurisdiction they apply to. Liquidity can be viewed across the group's bank accounts, and approvals can be reviewed company by company.
