## Workforce and payroll

ZoikoSuite presents workforce and payroll operations within its governed platform, including Payroll and Human Resources. Support for a particular payroll activity, filing or employment rule depends on the jurisdiction, configuration and released coverage.

## Payroll

Each payroll is prepared as a snapshot for an entity, a pay cycle and the jurisdictions involved, and it cannot be changed once it is finalized. Payroll tax is calculated with jurisdiction-aware rules, and each calculation keeps the rule basis it used. Anomalies, conflicts and missing evidence are raised as payroll exceptions before release.

## Human Resources

Human Resources keeps each employee's core record and lifecycle, together with compensation, benefits, leave and absence, employment contracts, organization structure and performance reviews. Employment contract changes are kept as new versions instead of overwriting earlier terms.

## Workforce compliance

Offboarding and termination workflows check the applicable employment rules before they proceed, and workforce compliance tracks employment-related obligations so gaps can be reviewed. ZoikoSuite does not give employment or payroll advice.
