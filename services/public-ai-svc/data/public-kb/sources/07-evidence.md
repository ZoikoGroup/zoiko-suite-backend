## Evidence

Evidence keeps filing evidence and preparation records together with the work they support. It also defines the evidence each governed action must carry before it may proceed, and checks whether that requirement is met.

## Audit-ready records

Because evidence is captured as work is done, reviewers and auditors can see what was prepared, by whom and when. Audit events are stored as append-only records linked to the action that produced them, and they can be searched by actor, entity, action, workflow or time range.

## Requirements before completion

Evidence requirements can name the documents, signatures and supporting records an action needs. An action cannot be finalized while required evidence is missing, and an action with no defined requirement is never reported as satisfied.

## Documents and evidence sets

Critical documents are stored with version history, access history, integrity controls and a retention policy. Evidence sets can be assembled for audit, regulatory, legal-discovery and compliance-review needs, and the history of workflow approvals and escalations is kept.

## Assurance and admissibility

ZoikoSuite describes evidence-linked workflows designed to preserve decision context, policies, approvals and supporting records. Independent assurance and legal admissibility require separate evidence and cannot be assumed from this description.
