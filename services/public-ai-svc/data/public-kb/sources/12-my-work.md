## My Work in the ZoikoSuite console

My Work is where each person sees what is waiting on them. It has three parts: Tasks & Approvals, Exceptions and Assigned Actions. Each item comes from the governed workflows in the other areas, so a person acts on it with the same approvals, authority and evidence rules that apply everywhere else.

## Tasks & Approvals

Tasks & Approvals lists the tasks and approvals waiting on you. An approval request is routed to the person whose role and delegated authority allow them to decide it, and the decision is recorded as evidence with who approved, when and under which authority.

## Exceptions

Exceptions lists governance exceptions raised to you for a decision: cases where a workflow could not follow its normal policy and needs a person to decide what happens next. The decision and its reason are recorded, so an exception never passes silently.

## Assigned Actions

Assigned Actions lists follow-up actions assigned to you, such as remediation steps after a review. What you can see and do depends on your role, entity scope and plan.
