## Migration and onboarding

Migration requirements depend on your existing system, data quality, controls and target workflows. The public guidance outlines the approach; a scoped review is needed before any timeline or compatibility commitment. The same approach applies whether you migrate from spreadsheets or from another business system.

## How migration is approached

Data brought in from other systems is normalized into ZoikoSuite's governed workflows and keeps a record of where it came from. Imported data goes through the same governance as other data before it is used in day-to-day operations.

## Validating the moved data

Validation runs check the moved data against an agreed acceptance bar, and any blocking issue fails the run. Accepting an exception is a recorded decision that needs a reason and a reference to the evidence supporting it, so the outcome can be reviewed later.

## Phased adoption

ZoikoSuite supports coexistence with a retained HRIS platform during phased adoption or a hybrid operating model, so a team does not have to move every system at once. Which external systems can be connected is confirmed from current published integration information.

## What a scoped review covers

A scoped review looks at your current systems, the quality of your data, the controls you rely on and the workflows you want to run. Timelines and compatibility are discussed only after that review.
