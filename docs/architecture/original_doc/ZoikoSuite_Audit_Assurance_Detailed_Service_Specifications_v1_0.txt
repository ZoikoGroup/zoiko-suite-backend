Executive doctrine — ZoikoSuite Audit &amp; Assurance is a controlled evidence, procedure, review and assurance workflow. It consumes authoritative financial and operational facts but never edits them; it preserves contradictory evidence; it separates client-provided information from accepted audit evidence; it freezes populations before testing; and it never allows automation or AI to make the professional audit opinion, materiality, significant-risk, sufficiency-of-evidence or independence judgment on behalf of the responsible auditor.

0. Critical Analysis &amp; Refined Architecture Decision
Audit must be read-only with respect to underlying accounting, tax, banking, payroll, operational and reporting facts. Corrections occur in the owning domain and flow back into the audit trail as new evidence or changed populations.
Engagement acceptance, independence, competence, ethics, materiality, significant-risk assessment, sufficiency/appropriateness of evidence and the audit opinion remain professional judgments of authorized humans under the applicable framework and firm methodology.
Client-provided information is not automatically audit evidence. Receipt, integrity, relevance, reliability and linkage to an audit procedure are separately modeled.
Audit populations are frozen, reproducible objects with source identity, query/filter version, watermark, control totals and hash. A live query cannot be used as the evidential population for sampling or conclusion.
Sampling is a governed design. Statistical and nonstatistical methods are supported, but selected items cannot be silently substituted and selection parameters cannot be changed after execution without invalidating the sample.
Contradictory or inconsistent evidence is preserved and surfaced. A workpaper cannot delete evidence merely because it does not support the final conclusion.
Workpapers are purpose/procedure/evidence/conclusion records with preparer/reviewer accountability. After engagement-file lock, additions are explicit addenda with who/when/why/effect—not in-place edits.
Findings, misstatements, control deficiencies, scope limitations and unresolved matters remain separate from management remediation. Closure never erases the original condition or its communication history.
Review and sign-off are version-bound. Protected changes to risks, procedures, evidence, conclusions, findings or report content invalidate downstream approvals and require re-review.
Professional standards are implemented as versioned Audit Framework Profiles and firm-methodology overlays. The platform does not hardcode one jurisdiction’s documentation-retention, sampling or reporting rules as universal law.
AI may summarize, cross-reference, detect anomalies, suggest procedures or draft narrative, but it cannot autonomously set materiality, classify a significant risk, select an audit opinion, conclude evidence is sufficient/appropriate, close a finding or sign a workpaper.
Audit exports are governed evidence packages with manifests, hashes, redaction and access controls—not unrestricted copies of production databases.

1.1 Non-bypassable Audit &amp; Assurance invariants
AUD services SHALL NOT write directly to the General Ledger, Tax Ledger, bank settlement facts, payroll results, AR/AP open items, source business documents or certified financial statements.
Every engagement SHALL bind to a versioned Audit Framework Profile, firm methodology/profile, reporting period, entity/group scope and responsible engagement leadership.
Engagement acceptance/continuance SHALL preserve evidence of preconditions, conflicts/independence evaluation and authorization; the platform SHALL NOT assert professional independence autonomously.
Materiality and performance materiality inputs SHALL be explicitly versioned and attributable; changes after dependent testing SHALL trigger impact analysis and potential invalidation/reperformance.
Every risk SHALL identify affected financial-statement area/assertion/process and planned response; significant-risk classification requires authorized human judgment.
Every population used for testing SHALL be frozen and reproducible with source identity, query/filter version, watermark, count/control totals and integrity hash.
Sampling SHALL preserve method, parameters, selection logic, random seed where applicable, selected items and evaluation results.
A selected sample item SHALL NOT be silently replaced. Unavailable items and alternatives SHALL be documented and evaluated.
PBC response receipt SHALL NOT automatically mark the item as sufficient, appropriate or accepted audit evidence.
Every evidence object SHALL preserve source, acquisition method, timestamp, integrity attributes and procedure/purpose linkage.
Contradictory, inconsistent or negative evidence SHALL remain visible to reviewers and cannot be deleted to support a preferred conclusion.
Every material workpaper SHALL identify preparer, completion time, reviewer(s), review date(s), procedures, evidence, results and conclusion.
Workpaper lock SHALL prevent ordinary in-place edits; subsequent changes SHALL be additive, attributable and preserve the prior content.
Protected change to a signed workpaper, finding, risk assessment, materiality input or report SHALL invalidate affected downstream sign-offs.
Reviewer and preparer conflicts SHALL be prevented according to the configured methodology and quality-management profile.
Findings and accumulated misstatements SHALL remain historically reconstructable even after remediation or closure.
Report release SHALL be blocked by unresolved mandatory review notes, required sign-offs, significant scope limitations or other configured completion gates.
Engagement archives SHALL be tamper-evident and subject to framework/jurisdiction/firm-policy retention and legal hold.
Exports SHALL apply least-necessary disclosure, tenant/entity authorization, confidentiality restrictions, redaction policy and integrity sealing.
AI SHALL remain advisory and SHALL NOT autonomously sign, approve, conclude, determine opinion, set materiality, close findings or declare evidence sufficient/appropriate.

8. Service Definition of Ready, Definition of Done &amp; Implementation Sequence
8.1 Definition of Ready
Applicable framework profile(s), jurisdictional overlays and firm methodology owner are identified.
Service authority boundary and source-system dependencies are agreed with Accounting/Data Governance/IAM owners.
Required object/state/event schemas exist in the canonical model and schema registry.
Retention/legal-hold/confidentiality classification is defined for each material audit object.
Permissions, dynamic SoD, client-contributor boundaries and reviewer roles are approved.
Negative-path acceptance tests and evidence requirements are mapped before implementation starts.
External connectors (confirmation providers, file sources, regulator/inspection exports) have security/residency contracts.
8.2 Definition of Done
Named commands, read models, event contracts and error semantics are implemented and contract-tested.
Frozen population and sample reproducibility tests pass at representative scale.
Workpaper lock, sign-off invalidation, contradictory evidence and post-lock addendum tests pass.
Client/auditor separation and cross-tenant access negative tests pass.
Archive, retention/legal-hold and export/redaction controls are verified end-to-end.
Every material conclusion can be reconstructed source → population/item → procedure → evidence → workpaper → review/sign-off.
Operational dashboards/runbooks cover failed extracts, evidence-integrity failures, stale PBCs, blocked sign-offs and archive/export failures.
Release certificate from ZS-QA-001 includes all mandatory audit-domain control evidence.
