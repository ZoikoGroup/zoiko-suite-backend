# Document Roadmap & Engineering Use

This pack instantiates the approved ZoikoSuite service-specification
standard for the authoritative Accounting & Record-to-Report domain. It
does not require 18 separately deployed microservices. It does require
that the authority, contracts, states, evidence, controls, service
identities, negative paths and operational obligations of ACC-01 through
ACC-18 remain separately testable and non-bypassable regardless of
physical deployment grouping.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Accounting truth flows in one direction: governed source fact →
  deterministic posting → append-only ledger → reconciliation/control →
  period close → trial balance/snapshot → reporting. No reporting,
  migration, consolidation or administrative component may write around
  that chain.**
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Critical Analysis & Refinements Incorporated

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Refinement**                      **Engineering consequence**
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------------
  One posting authority               ACC-04 is the only accounting translation
                                      authority. Source domains emit governed
                                      facts; they do not invent ledger entries.

  Ledger is truth, not workflow       ACC-05 accepts only committed postings
                                      from ACC-04. It has no generic user-write
                                      surface.

  Journal ≠ ledger entry              ACC-03 owns journal proposal/lifecycle.
                                      Posted accounting truth remains in
                                      ACC-05.

  Read models cannot become ledgers   ACC-15 Trial Balance and reporting
                                      projections are reproducible views over
                                      authoritative entries/watermarks.

  Snapshot before external reliance   Filed/signed/audited reporting should
                                      reference ACC-16 immutable snapshots
                                      rather than mutable live queries.

  Close is a control state machine    ACC-14 close consumes mandatory
                                      reconciliations and blockers; a UI
                                      checklist cannot override posting rules.

  Consolidation is book-separated     ACC-12 top-side/elimination adjustments
                                      post only to consolidation books and
                                      never rewrite entity statutory ledgers.

  Opening balances follow the kernel  ACC-17 migration accounting still
                                      validates mappings, control accounts,
                                      period and reconciliation before ACC-04
                                      posting.

  Corrections preserve history        Posted records are never edited in place;
                                      reversal/adjustment/supersession/reopen
                                      patterns create new governed facts.

  Framework-neutral core              The kernel stores policy/rule versions
                                      and supports multiple books;
                                      IFRS/GAAP/tax requirements are
                                      configuration/jurisdiction
                                      responsibilities, not hard-coded ledger
                                      branches.

# 2. Domain Constitution & Non-Negotiable Invariants

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **\#**                              **Invariant**
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  1                                   Every posted journal is balanced
                                      under the applicable book/currency
                                      balancing rule.

  2                                   No posted ledger line is edited or
                                      deleted in place.

  3                                   ACC-04 posting is idempotent at
                                      source-event + accounting-book +
                                      consequence level.

  4                                   A posting either commits its entire
                                      balanced consequence or commits
                                      nothing.

  5                                   Account, mapping, posting-rule, FX,
                                      period and accounting-policy
                                      versions used are persisted with
                                      the posting.

  6                                   Hard-closed periods reject ordinary
                                      posting; reopen is explicit,
                                      scoped, approved and evidenced.

  7                                   Control accounts cannot be bypassed
                                      by ordinary manual journals where
                                      policy restricts direct posting.

  8                                   Transaction, functional and
                                      presentation currencies remain
                                      distinct concepts.

  9                                   Consolidation-only entries never
                                      contaminate entity statutory books.

  10                                  Unmatched intercompany balances
                                      remain visible and are not silently
                                      eliminated.

  11                                  Trial balances reconcile to an
                                      explicit ledger watermark.

  12                                  Externally relied-on financial
                                      states reference immutable signed
                                      snapshots.

  13                                  Opening balances and migrated open
                                      items are separately reconciled to
                                      prevent double counting.

  14                                  Every reportable amount can be
                                      traced to ledger, journal, source
                                      event/document and governing
                                      versions.

  15                                  AI may classify/propose/explain but
                                      cannot become posting, close,
                                      certification or adjustment
                                      authority.

# 30. Prohibited Accounting Anti-Patterns

  **\#**                              **Prohibited pattern**
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  1                                   Source domain writes ledger lines
                                      directly

  2                                   Posting logic duplicated inside
                                      AR/AP/Payroll/Tax services

  3                                   Ledger entry edited/deleted in
                                      place

  4                                   Generic PATCH changes posted or
                                      closed accounting state

  5                                   Hard-coded accounts inside business
                                      code without mapping/version
                                      evidence

  6                                   Floating-point arithmetic for
                                      financially material amounts

  7                                   "Exactly once" broker claim used
                                      instead of business idempotency

  8                                   Partial posting accepted after one
                                      journal line fails

  9                                   Control account manually posted
                                      without explicit policy/authority

  10                                  Closed period reopened implicitly
                                      by admin/database change

  11                                  Unmatched intercompany silently
                                      eliminated

  12                                  Consolidation adjustment posted to
                                      statutory entity book

  13                                  Trial balance treated as an
                                      independent ledger

  14                                  External report generated from
                                      mutable live query instead of
                                      governed snapshot

  15                                  Migration loader bypasses posting

# 32. Final Architecture Decision

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **APPROVED DESIGN BASELINE --- ACC-01 through ACC-18 are logical
  accounting capabilities with one posting authority, one append-only
  ledger truth, explicit control/close gates, book-separated
  consolidation, immutable certified snapshots and source-to-report
  traceability. They may share runtime infrastructure, but no deployment
  choice may collapse or bypass these authority boundaries.**
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

This pack is build-ready when engineering can generate machine
contracts, schemas, posting rules, workflow/SoD policies, automated
accounting/control tests, runbooks and release evidence for each ACC
capability without inventing new accounting authority. Any divergence
requires an architecture decision record and explicit Finance
Architecture approval.
