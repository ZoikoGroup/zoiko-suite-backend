Executive doctrine: ZoikoSuite Assets, Inventory & Project Accounting is a controlled subledger layer. Physical assets, stock quantities, valuation, project costs and project revenue/WIP remain separate authoritative facts. On-hand quantities and carrying values are derived from immutable events and approved policy. No service in this domain writes the General Ledger directly, invents accounting policy, treats billing as revenue, or alters certified historic state in place.
[TABLE END]
[Heading1] 1. Architecture Decisions That Must Not Be Diluted
[TABLE START]
Decision | Engineering consequence
Physical identity and book value are different | AST-01 keeps stable asset/component identity; book-specific carrying state comes from AST-03 events + AST-02 depreciation and can differ by accounting book.
Asset economics are event-driven | Capitalization, improvement, impairment/revaluation, component replacement and disposal are append-only governed events; carrying values are never free-form editable balances.
Inventory quantity and inventory value are separate | INV-03 owns quantity movement truth; INV-04 owns monetary valuation. Neither can silently rewrite the other.
On-hand is a projection | Inventory quantity is reconstructed from immutable movements plus controlled count adjustments, not maintained as an independently editable balance field.
Commercial product is not inventory item authority | INV-01 owns stockkeeping/accounting attributes; BIZ-07 may own commercial catalog/offer data but cannot change valuation policy.
Valuation method is accounting-policy controlled | Cost formula/method is effective-dated by book/framework. IFRS restrictions or other-GAAP permissions are enforced by policy, not hardcoded globally.
Count variance is not a direct balance edit | INV-05 approves variance; INV-03 creates the adjustment movement; INV-04 values it; ACC-04 posts any accounting consequence.
Project cost capture does not double-post source costs | PRJ-02 attributes authoritative AP/payroll/inventory/asset costs to projects. Only incremental reclassification/capitalization effects produce new accounting events.
Project billing is not project revenue | AR owns issued invoices; PRJ-03 determines recognition/WIP/contract balance under approved policy; cash settlement remains Banking.
Project profitability is a read model | PRJ-04 cannot recognize revenue, post costs, edit WIP or change project lifecycle.
All accounting consequences route through ACC-04 | Subledger services emit governed accounting events; direct GL writes are prohibited.
Historic state must be reproducible | Every schedule, movement, valuation, count, project cost and recognition run preserves source identity, policy version, effective dates and correction chain.

[Heading1] 2. Domain Constitution & Non-Negotiable Invariants
[TABLE START]
# | Invariant
1 | Every authoritative record is tenant- and legal-entity-scoped before data access; cross-entity ownership is never inferred from physical location or project grouping.
2 | Asset physical identity is separate from book-specific accounting state; one asset may have multiple approved book profiles without one book overwriting another.
3 | Asset carrying values are derived from capitalization/subsequent events and depreciation schedules; direct value-field edits are prohibited.
4 | Asset components with materially different useful lives or policy treatment can be represented and depreciated separately.
5 | Applied asset events are immutable; corrections use reversal/supersession and preserve original evidence.
6 | A transfer of asset ownership between legal entities is not a metadata transfer; it follows approved intercompany/disposal-acquisition accounting.
7 | Depreciation is deterministic by asset/component/book/period and exact policy version; reruns are idempotent and population-frozen.
8 | Inventory item accounting attributes are separate from commercial catalog pricing/offers.
9 | Inventory on-hand quantity is derived from committed movements; direct quantity overwrites are prohibited.
10 | Every material movement preserves source identity, item, UOM, location, lot/serial where applicable, business/effective dates and idempotency identity.
11 | Negative inventory behavior is explicit policy; the system never silently allows negative stock because a projection is stale.
12 | Physical location/custody and legal ownership are separate facts.
13 | Inventory valuation uses effective-dated accounting policy and preserves method/cost-layer trace; late costs trigger controlled revaluation, not silent historical mutation.
14 | Accounting-framework constraints are policy-driven: for example an IFRS book cannot use a valuation method prohibited by the active IFRS policy profile.
15 | Inventory write-downs/reversals require approved evidence and remain distinct from physical quantity movement.
16 | Count populations and system quantities are frozen at a defined cutoff; in-flight movements are reconciled explicitly.
17 | Blind-count controls prevent unauthorized exposure of expected quantities where configured; material variance approvals are independent.
18 | Approved stock-count variances produce inventory movements; count services never edit on-hand balances directly.
19 | Financial projects/jobs are legal-entity scoped. Group programs reference separate entity projects rather than one cross-entity accounting aggregate.
20 | Project cost entries preserve authoritative source-domain identity and do not create duplicate source accounting.
21 | Project revenue recognition remains separate from customer billing, cash settlement and tax determination.
22 | WIP, unbilled revenue, contract asset/liability and deferred balances are policy-specific states; the platform does not treat them as interchangeable labels.
23 | Project estimates, progress measures and recognition methods are versioned; protected changes invalidate approval/certification.
24 | Closed-period corrections follow governed reopen or forward-correction rules and reperform affected depreciation/valuation/recognition controls.
25 | All subledger-to-GL controls and source-to-report lineage must remain reconstructable from evidence; AI cannot autonomously change carrying value, valuation method, count variance or revenue recognition.

[Heading1] 4. Shared Command, Error & Context Contract
[TABLE START]
Area | Required behavior
Context | Every protected command resolves tenant, legal entity, accounting book/basis, accounting period, jurisdiction/policy, currency, actor/workload, region/residency, correlation/causation and idempotency server-side.

[Heading1] 10. Inventory Valuation Method Governance
[TABLE START]
Policy area | Engineering rule
Method registry | Specific identification, FIFO, weighted average and other framework-permitted profiles are versioned methods. The engine does not globally assume one GAAP.
Accounting-framework constraints | Jurisdiction/accounting policy determines permitted methods. An IFRS profile rejects LIFO; another approved framework may permit methods not available under IFRS.
Method changes | Changes are future-effective or processed through a controlled conversion/revaluation/migration plan. Historic cost layers retain their original method identity.
Cost composition | Purchase, conversion, landed and other eligible costs are source-linked and policy-classified. Ineligible costs cannot be capitalized merely because they are available.
NRV / impairment-like inventory adjustment | Write-down/reversal uses approved evidence and policy. It does not change physical quantity.
Late-arriving cost | Late freight/landed cost or invoice correction creates controlled revaluation/reallocation and accounting correction according period state; closed-period history is not silently rewritten.

[Heading1] 17. Prohibited Domain Anti-Patterns
[TABLE START]
Anti-pattern | Why prohibited
Store asset net book value as an editable master field | Creates a second source of truth separate from asset events/depreciation.
One global depreciation schedule for all books | Different accounting/tax/management books may require different bases and policies.
Edit posted asset event | Destroys historical evidence and correction lineage.
Physical asset transfer changes legal owner automatically | Custody/location is not legal ownership; cross-entity transfers need governed accounting/legal treatment.
Maintain inventory on-hand as independently editable balance | Quantity must be derived from immutable movements and approved adjustments.
Let product catalog control inventory valuation | Commercial pricing/offer data is not inventory-accounting policy.
Hardcode FIFO globally | Platform must support framework-specific policy; methods are governed and effective-dated.
Use stock count to overwrite quantity | Count variance must create an auditable adjustment movement.
Project cost subledger reposts every source cost to GL | Creates duplicated expenses/assets and breaks source authority.
Treat invoice or cash receipt as revenue by default | Billing, cash and revenue recognition are separate facts.
Use “WIP” for every unbilled/contract balance | Inventory WIP, contract asset/liability and project WIP have different accounting meaning.
Allow analytics to alter project profitability inputs | PRJ-04 is a read model only.
Backdate into hard-closed period to “fix” subledger | Requires governed reopen or forward correction and re-reconciliation.
Let AI set impairment, valuation or recognition amount authoritatively | Material accounting outcomes require deterministic policy/evidence and accountable approval.
[TABLE END]
[Heading1] 18. Current Accounting-Framework Architecture Notes
[TABLE START]
IAS 16 Property, Plant and Equipment establishes principles for recognizing property, plant and equipment, measuring carrying amounts, and depreciation/impairment. ZoikoSuite therefore separates stable asset identity from book-specific cost, depreciation and subsequent-event history, and supports component-level schedules under approved policy.
[TABLE END]
[TABLE START]
IAS 2 Inventories requires inventories to be measured at the lower of cost and net realizable value and describes specific identification, FIFO and weighted-average cost formulas. The platform therefore treats cost formula as an effective-dated accounting-policy profile and must reject a method not permitted by the active framework; it does not hardcode one global method.
[TABLE END]
[TABLE START]
IFRS 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers recognizes revenue based on transfer of promised goods/services and distinguishes performance satisfied over time from point-in-time recognition. PRJ-03 therefore separates contract/billing facts from recognition, requires versioned progress evidence for over-time methods, and keeps contract assets/liabilities distinct from inventory WIP.
[TABLE END]
[TABLE START]
IAS 23 requires borrowing costs directly attributable to a qualifying asset to form part of that asset cost under its criteria; other borrowing costs are expensed. ZoikoSuite therefore models borrowing-cost capitalization as a controlled accounting-policy input rather than a free-form project-cost choice. IAS 36 separately governs impairment of many non-financial assets; impairment events require approved recoverability evidence and never arise from an AI guess.
[TABLE END]
Architecture references: IFRS Foundation — IAS 16 Property, Plant and Equipment; IAS 2 Inventories; IFRS 15 Revenue from Contracts with Customers; IAS 23 Borrowing Costs; IAS 36 Impairment of Assets. ZoikoSuite remains accounting-framework neutral: jurisdiction/accounting-policy packs determine the applicable GAAP treatment. This engineering standard is not accounting, tax or legal advice and is not itself a compliance certification.
[Heading1] 19. Final Architecture Decision
[TABLE START]
APPROVED BASELINE: ZoikoSuite Assets, Inventory & Project Accounting is implemented as an event-driven, multi-book and evidence-first subledger domain. Asset identity/book values, inventory quantity/value, and project cost/revenue/WIP remain separate governed facts. All material calculations are deterministic under versioned policy, all corrections preserve history, all accounting effects flow through ACC-04, and each subledger must reconcile to source populations and the GL before certification. The domain is build-ready only when duplicate movement/posting, closed-period correction, count independence, framework-specific valuation, revenue-recognition evidence and source-to-report negative paths are enforced in code and certified end-to-end.
