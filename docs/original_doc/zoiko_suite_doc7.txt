Executive doctrine

ZoikoSuite must preserve one governed chain of truth from commercial entitlement and organizational context through
connected-system facts, obligations, controls, workflow decisions, evidence, approvals, AI-assisted recommendations,
execution and audit -- without silently replacing the systems of record, qualified professional judgment, legal authority, or a
tenant's own accountability. No action may become commercially billable, operationally authoritative, compliance-relevant or
externally represented as complete because of stale configuration, unsupported jurisdiction, unverified source data, model
confidence alone, an unapproved automation, failed integration, missing evidence, or cross-tenant/cross-product
contamination.

REFINED FINAL -- COMMERCIAL, PRODUCT & ENGINEERING BASELINE

This standard is intentionally stricter than a pricing document or feature catalogue. It governs ZoikoSuite commercial truth,
tenant and legal-entity scope, capability entitlements, connected systems of record, compliance/control intelligence,
jurisdictional rules, evidence provenance, AI and automation boundaries, human approvals, integrations, reporting,
ZoikoSuite subscription billing, Zoiko One bundling, security, privacy, retention, testing and production acceptance. Where
the approved commercial or product record is incomplete, the system must fail closed rather than invent price, legal
coverage, compliance status, market availability or autonomous authority.

5. A. Commercial Account, Organization, Workspace & Legal Entity
Model

A1. What is the top-level tenant object?

Decision: ORGANIZATION is the commercial/product tenant. Workspaces and legal entities sit beneath it.
Commercial / product rationale: Enterprise customers may have multiple business units, jurisdictions and operational
workspaces without needing duplicate commercial identities.
Engineering rule: Persist organization_id, commercial_account_id, workspace_id and legal_entity_id separately. Do not infer
legal entity, region or subscription payer from the signed-in user.

A2. Can one organization operate multiple legal entities and business units?

Decision: Yes, when entitled and market-enabled.
Commercial / product rationale: Governance, evidence and reporting often differ by legal entity, employing entity, reporting
entity or operating unit.
Engineering rule: Every obligation, control, evidence item, workflow and report row carries the appropriate
organization/workspace/entity scope. Cross-entity aggregation is explicit and traceable.

A3. Can users belong to multiple organizations or workspaces?

Decision: Yes, with scoped membership.
Commercial / product rationale: Advisers, shared-service teams and group executives may need controlled cross-
organization access.
Engineering rule: Resolve actor + organization + workspace + entity + data class + permission on every request; deny by
default when scope is ambiguous.

A4. What is the commercial account?

Decision: The verified customer record that buys ZoikoSuite.
Commercial / product rationale: The buyer, payer and contracting entity may differ from operational users and entities being
governed.
Engineering rule: commercial_account stores legal customer name, billing/tax facts, contract/order references, processor
customer refs, billing_source, renewal and payment state. Operational data stays separate.

A5. Can workspaces be created without being billable?

Decision: Yes.
Commercial / product rationale: Pilots, internal controls, implementation workspaces and sandboxes may be legitimate non-
billable estates.
Engineering rule: Workspace charge authorization requires billing_classification + entitlement + catalog/contract + billable
event. Environment name or workspace age is never enough.

A6. What happens when a member is removed?

Decision: Access ends according to security policy; historical attribution remains.
Commercial / product rationale: Governance evidence must retain who approved, changed or reviewed past items.
Engineering rule: Deactivate membership and revoke sessions/tokens; do not delete actor IDs from audit history, evidence,
approvals or historical exports.

6. B. Plans, Packaging, Contracts, Trials, Entitlements & Upgrades

B1. What are the canonical public plans?

Decision: Whatever the current APPROVED pricing registry defines; this standard does not invent names.
Commercial / product rationale: The available product record confirms tiered pricing architecture but not a final price book in
this operating standard.
Engineering rule: Use stable internal plan_id and catalog_version_id independent of display label. Unknown or unpublished
mappings fail closed.

B2. What can a plan entitle?

Decision: Approved capability packs, user/entity/workspace limits, integrations, evidence volume, AI/automation allowances,
support/security options, market scope or contracted capacity.
Commercial / product rationale: ZoikoSuite is multi-dimensional; packaging must not force schema redesign when the
commercial model evolves.
Engineering rule: subscription_item supports metric_type, entitlement_set_id, limits, add-ons, market scope and contract
overrides. Telemetry alone cannot become a billable metric.

B3. Is a free plan or free trial assumed?

Decision: No.
Commercial / product rationale: Commercial truth must come from the approved catalog/evaluation program.
Engineering rule: Use EVALUATION_PROGRAM with duration, payment requirement, entitlement set, conversion policy and
expiry action. No automatic charge without approved terms and payment authorization.

B4. How do upgrades work?

Decision: Immediate or scheduled according to catalog/contract after commercial preview and required authorization.
Commercial / product rationale: Capabilities should not race ahead of payment, contract approval, market eligibility or
security prerequisites.
Engineering rule: Persist quote/preview, catalog version, effective_at, approver/customer consent and resulting entitlement
version. Activate only after required prerequisites succeed.

B5. How do downgrades work?

Decision: At renewal by default, subject to compatibility and retention checks.
Commercial / product rationale: A downgrade can invalidate integrations, entities, workflows, AI allowances or
retention/security options.
Engineering rule: Schedule downgrade; generate incompatibility report; block destructive remediation. Historical records and
evidence are never deleted merely to fit a lower plan.

B6. How are bespoke enterprise terms handled?

Decision: Through approved contract/order-form overlays, not hidden code switches.
Commercial / product rationale: Enterprise terms may modify quantities, markets, support, data residency, integration scope
and security commitments.
Engineering rule: Store contract_entitlement_overlay with effective dates, approval, legal reference, precedence and renewal
behavior. Every runtime entitlement decision is explainable to a catalog/contract source.

B7. Can AI or integration usage create surprise charges?

Decision: No.
Commercial / product rationale: Operational telemetry is not commercial consent.
Engineering rule: A usage meter becomes billable only when meter_definition + catalog rate/allowance + disclosure +
customer authorization + approved aggregation policy are active.

9. E. Obligation, Policy, Control & Compliance Model

E1. What is the core governance chain?

Decision: OBLIGATION  REQUIREMENT  POLICY/CONTROL  EVIDENCE/TEST  FINDING/EXCEPTION 
REMEDIATION  REVIEW/ATTESTATION.
Commercial / product rationale: This prevents "compliance" from becoming an unexplained dashboard color.
Engineering rule: Persist scoped, versioned objects and links. Every status must be traceable to source, jurisdiction/entity,
owner, evidence and review state.

E2. What makes an obligation applicable?

Decision: A versioned applicability decision based on jurisdiction, entity, activity, product/process, thresholds, dates and
approved source rules.
Commercial / product rationale: Applicability is contextual and changes over time.
Engineering rule: Store applicability_decision with source/rule versions, effective dates, facts used, actor/system,
confidence/uncertainty and review requirement. Unknown applicability does not default to NOT_APPLICABLE.

E3. Can a control be marked effective because it exists?

Decision: No.
Commercial / product rationale: Design existence and operating effectiveness are different.
Engineering rule: Separate DESIGN_STATUS and OPERATING_EFFECTIVENESS. Effectiveness requires test/evidence
under an approved methodology and review cadence.

E4. What does COMPLIANT mean?

Decision: Only a scoped, defined status produced by an approved evaluation rule; it is not a universal legal guarantee.
Commercial / product rationale: Overbroad labels create legal and product risk.
Engineering rule: UI/reporting must show scope, as-of date, source coverage, exceptions, evidence freshness and limitations.
Prefer "control status," "obligation status" or "assessment status" where appropriate.

E5. Can users override control or obligation status?

Decision: Only through governed exception/override workflow.
Commercial / product rationale: Manual edits without reason or expiry destroy assurance value.
Engineering rule: Override requires reason, authority, scope, expiry/review, compensating control where applicable and
immutable audit. High-risk overrides require second approval.

E6. How are attestations handled?

Decision: As signed/attributed assertions with scope and evidence, not automatic proof.
Commercial / product rationale: An attestation can be important but remains a representation by an identified actor.
Engineering rule: attestation stores statement/version, subject, period, signer, role, timestamp, evidence refs, expiry and
challenge/revocation state.

10. F. Regional Intelligence, Jurisdiction & Effective-Date Governance

F1. Can one rule apply globally?

Decision: Only when an approved global rule explicitly has that scope.
Commercial / product rationale: Business, tax, employment, privacy, reporting and regulatory requirements vary by
jurisdiction and entity.
Engineering rule: Every obligation/rule/policy has jurisdiction_scope, entity_scope, effective_from/to, source_version and
supersession links. "GLOBAL" is an explicit scope, not a default.

F2. How are legal/regulatory changes introduced?

Decision: Through governed source intake, review, impact analysis, version release and effective-date activation.
Commercial / product rationale: A new publication should not instantly rewrite tenant obligations without validation.
Engineering rule: Change pipeline: source_received  validated  mapped  reviewed  approved  scheduled/active.
Impacted tenants/processes are enumerated before activation.

F3. What if a market is unsupported or ambiguous?

Decision: Fail to unsupported/uncertain state and route appropriately.
Commercial / product rationale: Guessing jurisdictional applicability is unsafe.
Engineering rule: Use MARKET_UNSUPPORTED / JURISDICTION_UNCERTAIN states. Block definitive compliance claims
and high-impact automation until resolved.

F4. Can translated regulatory content be authoritative automatically?

Decision: No.
Commercial / product rationale: Translation can change legal meaning.
Engineering rule: Translation has approval tier, source-language reference, reviewer and allowed-use. High-impact use may
require approved localized source or human review.

F5. How are time-zone and legal-day rules handled?

Decision: From market/entity calendars and explicit policy.
Commercial / product rationale: Deadlines and effective dates can depend on local time, holidays and business-day
conventions.
Engineering rule: deadline_rule stores timezone, calendar, holiday source, cutoff semantics, rule version and exception path.
Never rely on application server timezone.

11. G. AI, Agentic Automation & Human Authority

AI GOVERNANCE DOCTRINE

ZoikoSuite may use AI to reduce analysis and operating work, but the platform must never confuse model capability with
organizational authority. The deterministic policy layer, source/evidence state, actor permissions, approved tool registry,
tenant automation policy and required human approvals outrank model preference.

G1. What may AI do by default?

13. I. Evidence, Auditability, Attestations & Control Testing

14. J. Documents, Records, Retention, Legal Hold & Exports

J1. How is retention determined?

Decision: By record type, tenant contract, jurisdiction, legal/regulatory need, source rights, privacy obligations and legal hold.
Commercial / product rationale: One global retention period is unsafe.
Engineering rule: Version retention_policy; every purge checks legal hold, record class, minimum/maximum rules and source-
system ownership before deletion/redaction.

J2. Does account cancellation delete records?

Decision: No automatic destructive deletion of governed records.
Commercial / product rationale: Financial, legal, HR and compliance evidence may need retention or export.
Engineering rule: Separate access state, commercial subscription state and data-retention state. Closure launches
retention/offboarding workflow.

J3. How does legal hold behave?

Decision: It blocks deletion/redaction actions covered by the hold until authorized release.
Commercial / product rationale: Ordinary lifecycle automation must not defeat preservation duties.
Engineering rule: legal_hold specifies scope, custodians/objects, authority, start, status and release approval. Hold checks
occur in deletion/export/migration paths.

J4. How are exports controlled?

Decision: By role, purpose, data class, recipient scope, source rights, legal hold and market rules.
Commercial / product rationale: Exports can become the largest uncontrolled data exfiltration surface.
Engineering rule: Export job uses preview/manifest, approval for restricted classes, watermark/classification where required,
asynchronous secure generation, expiry and audit event.

J5. Can generated Word/PDF packs imply certification?

Decision: Only when an approved assurance/attestation workflow permits the exact wording.
Commercial / product rationale: A professional-looking document can be mistaken for legal or audit certification.
Engineering rule: Template registry separates operational reports, evidence packs, attestations, certifications and drafts.
External assurance wording requires Legal/Compliance approval and authorized signatory.

customers stay on referenced version until governed transition.

U2. What is required for emergency overrides?

Decision: Named approver, reason, exact scope, expiry, customer/market impact, compensating controls and rollback/review
plan.
Commercial / product rationale: Emergency operation must not create permanent invisible governance drift.
Engineering rule: Override object is time-bounded, evented and cannot bypass immutable data, legal hold, tenant isolation or
prohibited actions.

U3. Who may change payment, provider, data-residency or high-impact execution
configuration?

Decision: Only tightly restricted roles under dual control where risk warrants.
Commercial / product rationale: These changes can redirect money/data or enable consequential actions.
Engineering rule: Step-up MFA, change ticket, dual approval, secret vault, destination/region allowlists where feasible,
notification and post-change reconciliation are mandatory.

32.1. What kill switches are required?

Decision: Granular controls for commercial charging, high-impact automation/tool actions, model/provider use, obligation/rule
activation, selected imports/syncs, outbound notifications/exports and public claim publication.
Commercial / product rationale: In an incident, stopping new harm must not require taking the whole platform offline.
Engineering rule: Kill switch is plane/domain/provider/tenant scoped, privileged, logged, approval-controlled, visible in
operations, and has reconciliation/restart procedure. It does not erase already-committed history.

32.2. What is safe degraded mode?

Decision: Preserve read/evidence/history where safe; stop actions whose authority or correctness cannot be proven.
Commercial / product rationale: Governance systems must fail predictably rather than fabricate continuity.
Engineering rule: Each service defines DEGRADED behavior: e.g., stale integration  display last verified fact with timestamp
but block high-impact action; model outage  deterministic workflow remains; evidence store outage  material approvals fail
closed.

35. Final CTO / Commercial Doctrine

ONE PLATFORM -- FIVE PLANES OF TRUST -- ZERO SILENT AUTHORITY

ZoikoSuite must make complex business operations governable, explainable and auditable without confusing visibility with
authority. Every commercial entitlement has a source. Every operational fact has an owner. Every obligation has jurisdiction
and time. Every control has a definition. Every evidence item has provenance. Every status has a rule. Every approval has
an accountable actor. Every AI output has boundaries. Every autonomous action has explicit authority. Every public claim is
tied to release truth. When price, market availability, source authority, evidence sufficiency, compliance status, provider
eligibility or execution authority is uncertain, the platform must refuse to guess.

 Commercial truth is versioned and governed; code and telemetry do not invent charges.
